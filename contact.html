BEFORE YOU ASK

Straight answers to common questions on timelines, pricing, and Kenyan settlement protocols.

01 How much does a website cost?
expand_more

Depends entirely on technical complexity. A high-converting single-page landing site starts from KES 25,000. Full web applications with bespoke backend pipelines, dashboards, and custom integrations start from KES 80,000. Hit our WhatsApp line for an itemized quote in minutes.

02 How long does it take?
expand_more

Most standard corporate and brand platforms are fully architected, deployed, and tested within 2 to 3 weeks. Larger platforms, bespoke web apps, and complex integrations ship in 4 to 6 weeks.

03 Do you accept M-Pesa?
expand_more

Yes. We accept Lipa na M-Pesa Buy Goods / Till, Paybill, direct bank wire (EFT/RTGS), and international USD settlement (Wise, Stripe, Wire). Our standard milestone model is 50% upfront commitment and 50% on final staged delivery.

04 Can you integrate M-Pesa on my site?
expand_more

Yes. We build native integrations using Safaricom's Daraja 2.0 API (STK Push, C2B, B2C, and real-time webhook confirmation callbacks), delivering instantaneous checkout workflows for your Kenyan userbase.

05 Do you work with clients outside Kenya?
expand_more

Yes. Over 40% of our production workload serves international founders and distributed organizations across North America, the UK, Europe, and UAE via asynchronous sprint reporting and scheduled video standups.
